## 1. Introduction

Phishing is a type of cyber attack in which attackers send fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, and OTPs.

The purpose of this activity is to identify phishing warning signs and understand how to protect personal information online.

## 2. Fictional Phishing Message Analysis

### Example 1: Fake Bank Alert

**Message:**

"URGENT! Your bank account will be blocked today. Verify your account immediately using the link below to avoid suspension."

**Warning Signs:**
- Creates urgency and fear.
- Threatens to block the bank account.
- Requests immediate action.
- Contains an unverified link.
- Does not provide a reliable way to verify the request.

**Safe Action:** Do not click the link. Contact the bank through its official website or customer support.

### Example 2: Fake Prize Notification

**Message:**

"Congratulations! You have won ₹5,00,000 in a lucky draw. Pay ₹999 as a processing fee to claim your prize."

**Warning Signs:**
- Unexpected prize notification.
- Requests money to receive a reward.
- Promises unrealistic benefits.
- Does not provide verifiable contest details.

**Safe Action:** Do not pay money or share personal information. Verify the offer through official sources.

### Example 3: Fake Job Opportunity

**Message:**

"Congratulations! You are selected for a work-from-home job with a salary of ₹50,000 per month. Send your Aadhaar details and bank information immediately to confirm your position."

**Warning Signs:**
- Offers an unusually high salary without proper details.
- Requests sensitive personal information.
- Creates pressure to respond immediately.
- Does not provide a verifiable company recruitment process.

**Safe Action:** Verify the company and job opening through its official website. Never share banking credentials or OTPs.

## 3. Common Phishing Warning Signs

1. Unknown or suspicious sender addresses.
2. Urgent or threatening language.
3. Spelling and grammatical mistakes.
4. Suspicious links and attachments.
5. Requests for passwords or OTPs.
6. Unexpected prizes or rewards.
7. Requests for money or confidential information.

## 4. Personal Security Checklist

### Password Security
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Avoid reusing passwords across websites.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Never share passwords or OTPs.

### Email and Message Security
- Verify the sender before responding.
- Avoid clicking unknown links.
- Do not download suspicious attachments.
- Report suspicious emails and messages.

### Device Security
- Keep the operating system and applications updated.
- Use trusted antivirus or security software.
- Enable device screen locks.
- Download applications only from trusted sources.

### Online Safety
- Use HTTPS websites for sensitive activities.
- Avoid entering personal information on unknown websites.
- Avoid sensitive transactions on unsecured public Wi-Fi.
- Review privacy settings on social media.
- Log out from shared devices.

### Account Protection
- Enable login alerts wherever available.
- Review account activity regularly.
- Use a password manager when possible.
- Change compromised passwords immediately.
